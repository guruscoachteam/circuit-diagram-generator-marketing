You can keep generating at the plan's per-credit overage rate, or upgrade to the next plan for a larger monthly allowance.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime from your billing settings; changes apply to your next billing cycle.

Do you offer education or team discounts?

Contact us from your billing settings for classroom or larger team pricing beyond the Studio plan.
