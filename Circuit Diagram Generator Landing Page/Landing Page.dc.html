Frequently asked questions

What is an AI circuit diagram generator?

It's a tool that turns a plain-English description or a hand-drawn sketch of a circuit into a clean, standards-correct schematic automatically, choosing real components and wiring them to spec.

How accurate are the AI-generated schematics?

The AI selects real parts with correct footprints and pin mappings, and every generated circuit can be simulated before you build it, so wiring or logic issues surface immediately.

Can I turn a hand-drawn sketch into a schematic?

Yes. Upload a photo of a sketch and the AI redraws it as a proper schematic with labeled components, ready to edit or simulate.

Can I simulate the circuit before building it?

Yes, every plan includes built-in simulation so you can verify behavior before ordering parts or etching a board.

What export formats are supported?

Export to KiCad and Eagle project files, plus PDF and SVG for documentation, lesson plans, or sharing.
